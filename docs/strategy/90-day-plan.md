# Fitsek 90-Day Plan

## Days 1–7
- Landing page live.
- Lead magnet complete.
- Email capture working.
- Instagram/Facebook Page ready.
- Analytics working.
- First 10 posts drafted.

## Days 8–30
- Publish daily via manual scheduling.
- Build email list.
- Test hooks and content pillars.
- Improve lead magnet promise and page conversion.
- Start weekly reports.

## Days 31–60
- Launch paid digital product with no-monthly-fee checkout.
- Add email sequence.
- Improve product based on user feedback.
- Track first sales.
- Test bundle/second product idea.

## Days 61–90
- Double down on winning content.
- Improve conversion funnel.
- Consider affiliate/referral system.
- Consider AI planner prototype only if product demand exists.
- Paid ads only if organic funnel conversion is proven.

## Milestones
- 30 days: 30 posts published, lead magnet live, 100+ email subscribers or improving traction evidence.
- 60 days: AUD $100–$500 revenue or strong waitlist/product interest.
- 90 days: repeatable content system, clear winning niche/topic, 500+ email subscribers, AUD $500–$2,000 cumulative revenue target.
