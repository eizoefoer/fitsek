# Fitsek Brand Basics

## Name usage
Use **Fitsek** as the brand name. Use “Fitsek” in headlines, bios, product names, filenames, and social profile names. Avoid pretending Fitsek is a personal coach or human founder.

## Positioning
Fitsek helps busy desk workers build muscle, lose fat, and simplify body recomposition with practical systems, trackers, and low-friction routines.

## Tagline options
1. Practical body recomposition for busy desk workers.
2. Desk-worker fitness systems that survive real work weeks.
3. Simple recomp trackers, routines, and meal templates for busy professionals.

## Bio
Practical body recomposition for busy desk workers. Strength, steps, protein, trackers, and weekly check-ins. Get the free 7-Day Desk Worker Recomp Reset ↓

## Tone
Simple, practical, evidence-aware, direct, encouraging. Avoid hardcore influencer bro-science, medical claims, hormone claims, fake transformations, and guaranteed outcomes.

## Colour/style direction
- Background: deep navy/black (`#07111f`, `#050b14`).
- Accent: mint (`#58f0a7`) and blue (`#6ab7ff`).
- Supporting: warm yellow for warnings/insights (`#ffd166`).
- Style: clean dashboards, checklist cards, tracker screenshots, dark-mode carousels, simple diagrams, desk-worker context.

## Logo direction
A simple “F” mark inside a rounded square/circle with mint-blue gradient. No mascot needed. Prioritise legibility as a social avatar.
