Purpose: reusable photorealistic Fitsek visuals for the public website and social approvals, while preserving the faceless brand and avoiding fake customer/testimonial/before-after claims.

## Research and compliance notes

- Existing repo rules: Fitsek is general fitness/wellness only; no medical claims, guaranteed outcomes, fake testimonials, fake before/after images, or generated people presented as real customers (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/compliance/meta-and-fitness-policy.md`).
- Existing social approval flow asks agents to generate or select faceless visuals from assets before user approval (`docs/operations/social-approval-workflow.md`).
- Fitness website pattern scan: modern fitness sites lean heavily on photography and brand story; for Fitsek the safe adaptation is faceless, context-led photography around desk schedules, movement, meal prep, and product/tracker context rather than transformation/body-proof imagery.
- Meta health/wellness policy search result noted risk around ads that imply negative self-perception for diet/weight-loss products; the image set avoids body-shaming, personal-attribute callouts, before/after layouts, and outcome promises.
