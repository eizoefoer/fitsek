# 12-Week Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Baseline and consistency (Weeks 1–2)
Set baseline steps, protein anchors, strength sessions, and weekly review.

## Phase 2 — Build the repeatable week (Weeks 3–6)
Progress strength volume slowly, create default meals, stabilise walking triggers, and reduce missed-day friction.

## Phase 3 — Adjust one lever at a time (Weeks 7–10)
Use body trend, training log, sleep, and adherence to decide small changes. Avoid overreacting to daily scale noise.

## Phase 4 — Consolidate and next cycle (Weeks 11–12)
Review wins, identify bottlenecks, choose maintain/cut/recomp focus, and plan the next 12 weeks.
