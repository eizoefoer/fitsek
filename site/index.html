FAQ

Is this medical advice?

No. Fitsek is general fitness and wellness education. Speak with a qualified professional before changing training, nutrition, or activity if you have a medical condition, injury, or concern.

Do I need a gym?

No. The free reset starts with desk-worker-friendly walking and simple strength options. The full product will include gym and home variations.

Will Fitsek make guaranteed fat-loss claims?

No. Fitsek helps you build a repeatable system. Results vary by individual, consistency, nutrition, training history, sleep, and many other factors.

Is this a SaaS?

Not yet. Fitsek starts with digital products and trackers. SaaS comes later only if the audience proves demand.
